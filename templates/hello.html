{# ── Transparency pitch ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Transparent by design

Every number on your dashboard links to the inputs that made it and the cited factors behind it.

  • 📜 DESNZ emission factors — the same UK government figures used in official greenhouse-gas reporting. Source cited on every result.
  • 🔎 Pinned calculations — your historical results are locked to the factors in use when you entered them. New factor releases never silently change old data.
  • 📄 Browse the catalogue — every formula and every emission factor is publicly readable without signing up.
  • 🏠 Household data stays yours — no selling, no ads. Invite housemates to share one account.
